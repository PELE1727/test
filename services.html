SERVICES

MINING

Mining is the extraction of geological materials and minerals from the surface of the Earth. Cassiterite and coltan are key minerals found in pegmatite ore hosting in metasedimentary rocks.

LABORATORY WORK

Mining laboratory work involves scientific testing and analysis of geological samples to determine chemical composition and quality control using XRF methods.

MINERAL PROCESSING

Mineral processing uses physical and chemical methods to separate valuable minerals from raw ore and waste rock, increasing grade and concentration.

MARKET

Metal and mineral prices are influenced by supply and demand, production costs, economic conditions, and global commodity markets.
